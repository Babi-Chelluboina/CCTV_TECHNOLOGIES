HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently asked questions

Not sure where to start? Here are a few things customers commonly ask before choosing a system.

Ask us a question >
How many cameras do I need for my property?

It depends on the areas you want to cover, camera placement and the level of detail you need. We can discuss your layout and suggest a suitable setup.

Can I view my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Compatible CCTV systems can support remote mobile viewing. The setup depends on the camera, recorder, network and app compatibility.

Should I choose a DVR or an NVR?

DVR systems are generally used with compatible coax cameras, while NVR systems record from IP network cameras. Existing cabling and camera choice help determine which fits.

Can you help with inverter and battery selection?

Yes. Share the appliances you want to power and the backup time you need, and we can help discuss a compatible system.

RECORDING & PLAYBACK

DVR & NVR Recorders

See recorder types, how they connect to cameras, and what to consider when choosing a system.

FOR COAX / ANALOG HD CAMERAS

DVR · Digital Video Recorder

Connects to compatible wired cameras, commonly over coaxial cable. Multi-format DVRs may support AHD, TVI, CVI or CVBS; confirm recorder and camera formats match.

  • Useful when coax cabling is already installed
  • Camera video connects directly to DVR inputs
  • Check channel count, resolution and hard drive support
FOR IP / NETWORK CAMERAS

NVR · Network Video Recorder

Records digital video from compatible IP cameras over a network. Some NVRs include PoE ports to power cameras through Ethernet; others need a separate PoE switch.

  • Designed for IP camera network systems
  • Confirm camera compatibility and network bandwidth
  • Check PoE ports, channel count and storage capacity
Multi-format DVR

Supports a mix of camera formats on select models. Inputs and supported resolutions vary.

PoE NVR

Models with built-in PoE can simplify wiring by carrying network data and camera power over Ethernet.

Choosing a recorder? Tell us how many cameras you need, whether your wiring is coax or Ethernet, and how many days of recording you want. We can help match the cameras, recorder and storage.

FIND THE RIGHT CAMERA

Explore CCTV Cameras

Compare common camera styles and choose an Analog DVR or IP network system for your property.

ANALOG / HD DVR

Dome camera

Compact ceiling or wall mount for homes, shops and indoor areas. Typical HD analog systems use coaxial cable and a DVR.

Good for: entrances, counters and covered spaces
ANALOG / HD DVR

Bullet camera

Visible, directional housing for outdoor walls and long approaches. Select weather protection and night vision to suit the location.

Good for: gates, driveways and building perimeters
IP NETWORK

IP bullet camera

Network camera that sends digital video to an NVR or compatible network system. Many models offer PoE, higher resolution and smart detection.

Good for: outdoor coverage and detailed views
IP NETWORK

Turret / eyeball camera

Low-profile adjustable camera for ceilings or walls. Pair with an NVR; PoE can carry power and data on one cable when supported.

Good for: shops, verandas and general area views
IP NETWORK · PTZ

360° robot / PTZ camera

Motorized pan, tilt and zoom lets an operator turn the view remotely and zoom in on areas of interest. Many speed domes can pan continuously through 360°; movement range and zoom depend on the model.

Good for: yards, parking areas and wide outdoor spaces
ANALOG / HD DVR · PTZ

Analog 360° speed dome

PTZ-style motorized control for compatible HD analog DVR systems. Check that the camera and recorder support the same control and video formats.

Good for: large areas where a DVR system is preferred
IP NETWORK

Compact network camera

Small network camera styles can suit doorways and indoor corners. Depending on model, features may include remote viewing, two-way audio or onboard storage.

Good for: rooms, reception areas and small spaces
